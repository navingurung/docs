## Step 1: Install Xcode

1. Go to https://developer.apple.com/download/all and sign in with an Apple ID.
2. Search **Xcode 26.6** and download **Xcode 26.6 Apple silicon.xip** (Intel Mac: use **Universal.xip**).
3. Double-click the `.xip` to extract it, then move `Xcode.app` to `/Applications`.

> ⚠️ Don't use the App Store version. It installs Xcode 27, which isn't supported by our current Expo SDK (56).
